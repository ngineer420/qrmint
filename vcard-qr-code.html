About the vCard QR code format

This tool builds a standard vCard 3.0 record — the same contact-card format used by Contacts apps, email clients and CRMs — and encodes it directly into the QR code. When someone scans it, their phone recognizes the vCard data and offers to add it as a new contact immediately, with every field filled in correctly.

What should I include?

A name plus one way to reach you (phone or email) covers most uses. Add organization and title for a business-card replacement, and a website if you want a scan to double as a portfolio link. Leave anything blank you'd rather not share — the QR code only encodes the fields you fill in.

Frequently asked questions

Will this work as a digital business card?

Yes — print it on a card, badge, or slide, or just show it on a screen. Anyone with a phone camera can scan and save your details in one tap.

Does it support multiple phone numbers or emails?

This generator keeps it to one of each for a clean, reliably-scannable code. For multiple contact methods, list a primary number and add the rest in a linked website or text QR code instead.

Can I put a company logo in the centre?

Yes — pick an image under Center logo, size it between 10% and 25% of the code, and give it a plate if you want clean space around it. Error correction locks to H so the code survives the covered modules, and the code is decoded again after every change so you know the card still scans before it goes to the printer.

Is my contact information stored anywhere?

No — the vCard is built and encoded entirely in your browser. QR Mint never sees or stores what you type.
